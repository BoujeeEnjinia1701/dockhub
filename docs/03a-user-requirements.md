# DockHub user requirements

What the people who use, host, service and regulate a DockHub station need, written from their side. They come from Tables 1 and 2 of the problem statement (DKH-PRB-001) and are desk research until the co-design sessions the problem statement lists have been run with a partner (Los Deliveristas Unidos is the first candidate, DKH-DEC-001). They score the concept options (DKH-OPT-001), and each product requirement in DKH-PRS-001 traces to one of them (DKH-RTM-001). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Usability | Fast swap | I put in my flat battery and take a charged one in about a minute, in a hurry, with gloves, at night | High | App-based delivery rider (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | A charged battery is there | When I arrive in a busy hour there is a charged battery, for 20 or more riders a day | High | Delivery rider; courier fleet operator |
| UR-003 | Safety | No fire danger | The station does not set fire to the street, the building or the riders, and if a battery fails the harm stays inside it | High | City fire safety department; host; rider |
| UR-004 | Environment and durability | Works outdoors all year | It works in rain, dust, sun, road salt and from -10 to 45 C | High | Host; transport department |
| UR-005 | Interoperability | Uses the open battery | It takes SwapCell packs as they are, with no change to the pack | High | Fleet operator; SwapCell project |
| UR-006 | Safety | Stays up in a storm | It stays upright in a 30 m/s gust with its canopy | High | Transport department; host |
| UR-007 | Security | Hard to rob or tamper with | A battery is released only to a valid user who returned one; the cabinet resists casual attack | Medium | Host; fleet operator |
| UR-008 | Usability | Everyone can use it | Controls are reachable and readable by people of all heights and abilities and in the dark | Medium | Transport department; rider |
| UR-009 | Logistics | Small and easy to power | It takes little sidewalk and one ordinary circuit | Medium | Host; transport department |
| UR-010 | Privacy | No watching me | No camera or microphone; the station knows a battery, not who I am; it keeps working offline | Medium | Rider; city |
| UR-011 | Maintenance | Quick to mend | One technician mends any part with hand tools in a short visit | Medium | Technician; fleet operator |
| UR-012 | Cost | Affordable to build | A cooperative, fleet or small business can afford to build one | Medium | Fleet operator; cooperative |
| UR-013 | Environment and durability | Some of it from the sun | Part of the charging energy comes from the canopy | Low | Transport department |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory, interoperability, security, privacy. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
